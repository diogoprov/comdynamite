| Level of organisation | Individuals. Individual-based model of two species in three patches. | Section 1 (last paragraph); Section 2; Fig. 1 |

## 3. Leibold et al. 2019, PNAS 116: 2612-2617, doi:10.1073/pnas.1808615116 (confirmation only)

Confirmed from the main text on PMC (the full extraction lives in the header of
`data-raw/replication_leibold2019.R`):

- Two models: a deterministic patch occupancy model (c = 2, m = 1) and a spatially explicit individual-based model. (Methods, "Patch Occupancy Model", "Spatially Explicit Individual-Based Model")
- IBM: 10 x 10 grid, two patch types, two species, one multilocus trait with a different optimum per patch type, zero initial standing variation, mutation at bi-allelic loci, carrying capacity per patch, sexual or asexual reproduction, random extinction of whole patches, niche tolerance omega. (Section "Spatially Explicit Individual-Based Model of Community Assembly"; Fig. 2 legend)
- Defaults: sexual reproduction, patch extinction P_ext = 0.01, dispersal Nm = 10 individuals per patch per generation, 10 replicates, 10 000 generations, all patches filled at the start with the pre-adapted species. (Methods)
- Trait classes used in variation partitioning: 0.35-0.45, 0.45-0.55, 0.55-0.65; edge patches excluded. (Methods, "Variation Partitioning")

One point to check against the SI: the Methods state that "one generation equals 10 iterations
of mortality, reproduction, and dispersal". The replication script treats one step as one
generation and applies P_ext = 0.01 per step. The main text does not say whether P_ext is per
iteration or per generation. Not verified here; it changes the disturbance rate by a factor of 10.

Values that come from SI Table S2 (10 diploid loci, mu = 1e-4, omega = 0.2, K = 400, rho = 2)
were not re-checked in this pass.
