# comdynamite: Process-Based Metacommunity Simulations with Phylogenetic Structure

Simulates metacommunity dynamics with the process-based model of
Thompson et al. (2020)
[doi:10.1111/ele.13568](https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.13568) :
density-independent growth that depends on the match between each
species' environmental optimum and the local environment, Beverton-Holt
competition, Poisson demographic stochasticity, and emigration and
immigration among sites. Landscapes can be spatially implicit, with
uniform dispersal, or explicit grids with an exponential dispersal
kernel and a spatially autocorrelated environment, and the environment
can change over time. Species traits (environmental optima, niche
breadths and maximum growth rates) can carry phylogenetic signal, and
interspecific competition can be structured by relatedness. The model
was implemented from the published equations.
